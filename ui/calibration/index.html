What calibration does

It teaches Maps++ where your character sits on this map's image. You show it two places you can identify both in game and on the minimap, and it works out the rest.

1 · Capture two points

  1. Stand somewhere you can recognise on the minimap — a shop door, a bridge, a corner.
  2. Move the mouse to that same spot on the minimap.
  3. Press Ctrl+Alt+1.
  4. Walk somewhere diagonally away and repeat with Ctrl+Alt+2.

The capture reads where your mouse is over the minimap, which is why it is a keyboard shortcut — clicking a button here would move the mouse off the point you are capturing.

Adding a new map

Drop the zone's image into the maps\ folder named after the zone's map file — MAP301.jpg and so on. The name Maps++ is looking for appears at the top of this window while you stand in that zone.
