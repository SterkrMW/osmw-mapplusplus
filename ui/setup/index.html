Welcome to Maps++

Maps++ runs beside MythWar Online and adds:

  • map A detailed minimap with your live position, points of interest and your party.
  • rocket_launch One-key launching, logging in and arranging when you play several characters.
  • tune An Action ring and shortcuts for the things you do in every fight.

How do you play?

Nothing here is permanent: Help → Setup guide in the tray menu brings this back, and Settings has every option.

Where is the game?

Maps++ needs main.exe to launch clients. The minimap works without it.
